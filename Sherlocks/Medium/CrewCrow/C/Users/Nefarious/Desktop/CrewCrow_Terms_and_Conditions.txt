CrewCrow Team Terms and Conditions

1. Confidentiality
All information and communications within the CrewCrow team are strictly confidential. Unauthorized disclosure will result in immediate expulsion.

2. Code of Conduct
Members must act professionally and respectfully. Misconduct will not be tolerated.

3. Communication Tools

Zoom: Use of Zoom for all meetings is mandatory due to its secure, end-to-end encryption.
Encrypted Email: Use only encrypted methods for email.
Secure Messaging Apps: Use approved encrypted messaging apps.

4. Data Security
Ensure all data is stored securely with strong passwords and encryption.

5. Compliance
Follow all instructions and guidelines provided by the leadership team.

6. Operational Conduct
Keep operational details confidential and report any security breaches immediately.

7. Use of Technology
Regularly update software, use VPNs, and maintain digital hygiene.

8. Consequences
Violations will lead to disciplinary action, including expulsion.

9. Amendments
The leadership team can amend these terms at any time. Members will be notified of changes.

By being a member of CrewCrow, you agree to these terms and conditions.
